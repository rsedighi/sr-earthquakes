Bay Tremor App Store Screenshots

Where they are:
- Full set (12): iphone/app-store-assets/screenshots/iphone-6.7/
- Full zip: iphone/app-store-assets/screenshots/baytremor-app-store-screenshots.zip
- Selected pack (5): iphone/app-store-assets/screenshots/selected/

How to upload:
1) Open App Store Connect -> My Apps -> BayTremor
2) App Store -> iOS App -> (your version)
3) Scroll to Screenshots
4) Upload the PNGs from iphone-6.7 (or the 'selected' 5-pack)

Important:
- You currently have ONLY 6.7" class screenshots (1284x2778 and 1320x2868). This is usually sufficient.
- If App Store Connect also asks for 6.5" screenshots, take them on Simulator: iPhone 11 Pro Max (1242x2688) and drop them into iphone/app-store-assets/screenshots/iphone-6.5/
